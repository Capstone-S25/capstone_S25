\section{Start from observed failure modes}
The strategy results motivated a controlled temporal experiment instead of another large undirected visual fine-tune. An error audit exports 117 deliberately selected severe/source-balanced cases across all five targets. Provisional notes distinguish low spatial detail, misleading crops, confusable actions, unsupported duration and item/owner evidence. This is a review queue for investigation, not an independent or representative statistical estimate of error causes. No reviewed test mistake is silently corrected into a favorable new label.

For CCTV smoking the median largest annotated smoker is only 11.2 pixels tall after 224-pixel letterboxing. In 71.8\% of held-out positive frames, all smokers are below 12 pixels. The previous automatic crop loses at least half of every target in 40.2\% of positive frames. Multiple person/full-upper-body crops reduce that coverage failure to one of 393 frames (.254\%). Detector resolution and crop policy change together, so this is a geometric coverage result rather than an isolated causal accuracy gain.

The item audit exposes a separate bottleneck: the COCO detector/association pipeline matches none of 136 sampled CAVIAR leaving-item reference boxes at IoU .5. A classifier cannot recover reliable item state if its automatic upstream item proposals are absent. This training-camera finding motivates a pre-score Grounding DINO geometry amendment with unchanged CLIP visual representations.

\section{Video preparation and identity repair}
The preparation covers 936 actual video files: 906 HMDB clips, 17 historical continuous recordings and 13 MEVA sources. Static smoking/photo examples also receive recording identities for bookkeeping, but those identifiers are not additional decoded surveillance videos. Continuous sources are decoded to real indices at 2 Hz; HMDB uses 16 sequentially decoded uniform views. Twenty-second windows end at the actual decision timestamp, with no decisions before a 20-second warmup. Unknown intervals use target $-1$ and never become normal examples.

The initial prepared modeling pool has 8,941 examples. Verification completes/repairs inherited pixel-hash fields in separate canonical manifests without changing labels, views, timestamps or splits. The known-source guard then finds a title-part alias: The Departed Part 1 and Part 2 cross train/development under raw prefixes. Ten training clips are purged, leaving 8,931 modeling examples; no test clip is removed. Earlier fits remain under \path{prefix_split_diagnostic/}, while all final selections use refitted guarded data.

The combined historical/new input verification covers 25,452 distinct view-image paths and no cross-fold decoded-pixel conflicts. The source guard covers explicit part/pt/clip/scene/episode suffixes; it does not independently verify every movie or actor identity. The new title grouping is therefore an improved safeguard with a stated limit.

\section{Automatic detection and causal tracking}
The common COCO detector is Faster R-CNN ResNet50 FPN v2, with minimum image size 640, maximum 1024, low confidence .2 and new-track threshold .5. Person and backpack/handbag/suitcase labels feed a documented Hungarian IoU/velocity association baseline. It retains tracks across gaps up to two seconds. Association can use IoU at least .05 or small predicted-center distance; velocity is smoothed from past displacements.

Stationarity originally resets when an observed center moves more than .015 normalized frame distance from its anchor. Ownership is a proximity hypothesis: an item must have a near person candidate observed for two seconds before association. Distance uses the item center relative to a person's rectangle normalized by person height. An owner becomes absent when missing or sufficiently far; the established relation is retained after departure. There is no appearance ReID or independent ownership truth. The algorithm is not ByteTrack.

The 18 geometry channels include clipped person/item counts, maximum age, stationary duration, owner-absence duration, owned-item fraction, presence, speed, minimum distance, area, detection confidence and visible-owner fraction. Source boxes do not choose tracks or inference crops. Missing detections affect the actual final output rather than being repaired with oracle annotations.

Grounding DINO tiny is pinned to revision \texttt{a2bb814dd30d776dcf7e30523b00659f4f141c71}, with a recorded safetensors hash. The fixed query is:
\begin{lstlisting}
person . bag . backpack . suitcase . cardboard box . package .
\end{lstlisting}
Box threshold .25, text threshold .20, class-wise NMS .5 and maximum item area .2 are fixed. A private label 1001 denotes the generic bag/package class. Its new-track threshold is .25. Custom kernels are disabled and inference is float32. Geometry changes while the existing CLIP image features remain fixed, isolating this ablation.

Against partial CAVIAR item references, DINO tracks recover 66/136 (48.5\%) at IoU .5; person reference recall rises from 36.0\% to 67.2\%. The references omit other carried bags, so unmatched item predictions cannot be called proven false positives or used as item precision.

\section{Frozen visual encoders and automatic regions}
This pilot uses frozen pretrained CLIP encoders, distinct from the six earlier visual fine-tunes. Each image supplies full-scene features, automatic region mean/max, and optional geometry. Up to four persons each contribute contextual whole-body and upper-55\%-body crops, plus up to two item/observed-owner contexts. This upper-body policy is a geometric heuristic, not a validated face/hand detector. Scene fallback is preserved when detections are absent.

Each backbone encodes 137,674 scene/crop views over the 25,452 input images in float32 with TF32 disabled. Frozen arrays preserve paths, full/crop features, geometry, region boxes, region-image indices and person flags. The extraction does not use anomaly labels to choose inference regions. Cached arrays are loaded once into memory for fitting/replay; repeatedly decompressing the same NPZ for every row was avoided.

\section{Fifty final candidate heads}
Each encoder fits 14 smoking and 11 unattended candidates, for 50 saved checkpoints total. Scene and multiple-region linear baselines use fixed $C=1$. Causal GRUs project inputs through layer normalization and 128-unit GELU/dropout .2, then use a 64-unit GRU and linear output. Region GRUs are compared with and without the 18 geometry features, across seeds 42/43/44. Relative elapsed time and inter-view gaps are explicit channels, with packed variable-length sequences preserving causal padding semantics.

AdamW uses learning rate .0005 and weight decay .01; heads train up to 60 epochs with patience eight, batch 128 and gradient clipping one. Task/source/class-balanced sampling is retained. Checkpoints and routes maximize worst development-source AP, with mean AP as tie-breaker. Static examples and variable-length action clips remain separate supported input types, not fabricated CCTV sequences.

Smoking additionally compares a per-person multiple-instance linear head, max-pooled over automatic person regions/views. Training-camera COCO annotations supervise 1,460 matched automatic regions, including 277 positives, at IoU at least .3; ambiguous matches are omitted. The loss combines weak scene/clip BCE with .5 times the matched-region BCE. No test box chooses an inference region.

The hard-negative version starts mining at epoch four using training normals within each source, upweighting difficult cases without changing original class/source mass. Both person variants use three seeds. A checkpoint selected before epoch four has not benefited from mining merely because its run name includes hard negatives. SigLIP has such a recorded early-selected case. This metadata is retained in the appendix.

MobileCLIP's selected person-supervised hard-negative route raises retrospective CCTV AP from the previous bundle's .690 to .725, while SigLIP's selected region GRU drops .722 to .688. Both do well on adapted movie/web clips, but the SigLIP selected route's .778 AP trails its own .888 scene-only pilot baseline. The previous bundle on the same 205 HMDB cases uses eight views and no HMDB training; the pilot uses 16 views and 530 grouped training clips. MobileCLIP .697 to .908 and SigLIP .762 to .778 therefore combine data/method changes. The scene baseline shares the new pilot's training data/views and is the better controlled reference. Neither establishes temporal CCTV generalization because the CCTV test remains static.

\section{Completion and recovery}
One concurrent SigLIP fit exhausts VRAM while the detector batches images. It is rerun after the detector exits; no feature extraction or labels change. Final verification replays 99,528 predictions, checks all 50 candidate selections and saved heads, and exercises four live encoder/ROI cases. Maximum saved-head difference is about $1.9\cdot10^{-6}$ and live-feature difference about $4.0\cdot10^{-7}$. At completion, 171 tests pass. A real checkpoint-backed replay CLI is available, but it reuses cached features and is not advertised as an arbitrary-video streaming application.
